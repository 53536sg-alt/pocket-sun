How to play

You are the Sunkeeper. Your little sun keeps the dark at bay, but it cannot wake a world on its own.

  • ✦Gather stardust drifting above the surface.
  • ◎Stand by a sleeping beacon and press E to spend 3 stardust. It blooms into light.
  • ◈Gloom creatures hate the light. Keep moving, and sprint when they get close.
  • ◇Light every beacon to open the gate. Follow the beam to the next planet.
